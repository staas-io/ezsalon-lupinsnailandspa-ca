ABOUT US

LUPINS NAILS AND SPA

Located conveniently in Stratford, PEI C1B 4A1, LUPINS NAILS AND SPA is proud to be one of the best nail salons in the area. We wish to bring our beloved customers a happy time when using our services.

 

At LUPINS NAILS AND SPA, the products we use are from famous brands in France and US to bring a quality nail set with high durability, long shelf life, ensuring you will be satisfied with the quality of service when you come to us. Our nail salon offers Manicures, Pedicures, Waxing, Eyelashes, and Brows Services with excellent service and comfort in a relaxing environment. Our high standards in quality products, sanitation, and exemplary client care will ensure that you will have a good time with us.

 

Especially, We take utmost care in cleanliness and professionalism. Our implements are medically sterilized and disinfected after each use. Buffers and files are used only once and then discarded. All pedicure procedures are done with liner protection.
